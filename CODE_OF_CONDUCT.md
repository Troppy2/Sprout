# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Sprout to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, background, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes, apologize to the people they affected, and learn from them.
- Act in the interest of the whole community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without their permission.
- Using Sprout's issue tracker or discussions to plan misuse of the tool against people or sites (for example, scraping sites that forbid it, or getting around bot protection).
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and chat) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (the maintainer's profile at https://github.com/Troppy2). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and issues that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone who does. The response depends on how serious the behavior is and whether it is repeated.
